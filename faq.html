Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I place an order?
Simply browse our products, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Your order will be sent to us via WhatsApp for confirmation.
2. What payment methods are available?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) and bank transfers. Payment details will be discussed during order confirmation.
3. Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes, Cash on Delivery is available for all regions we deliver to.
4. How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery usually takes 3-5 business days depending on your location.
5. Can I cancel an order?
Yes, you can cancel your order by replying to the WhatsApp confirmation message before the order is dispatched.
6. How can I contact support?
You can contact us via the Contact Us page, or simply message us on WhatsApp using the button on our site.
7. Is there free delivery?
We occasionally run promotions offering free delivery. Check our banners or subscribe to our newsletter for updates.
8. How do I track my order?
Once dispatched, we will send you a tracking link via WhatsApp.
